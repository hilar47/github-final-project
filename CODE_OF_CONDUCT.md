# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who participates in this project.

We expect all contributors, maintainers, users, and community members to communicate and behave respectfully, regardless of background, experience level, or role in the project.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and considerate toward others.
* Using welcoming and inclusive language.
* Giving constructive feedback.
* Accepting different opinions and perspectives.
* Focusing on ideas and contributions rather than individuals.
* Respecting different levels of experience and knowledge.
* Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
* Trolling, insulting, or deliberately provoking others.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Publishing private or personal information without permission.
* Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
* Deliberately disrupting discussions or project activities.
* Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or community setting.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers.

When reporting an issue, provide as much relevant information as possible, such as:

* What happened.
* When and where it happened.
* The people involved.
* Any relevant screenshots, links, or other evidence.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may take appropriate action, including:

* Asking someone to stop inappropriate behavior.
* Removing inappropriate content.
* Issuing a warning.
* Temporarily restricting participation.
* Permanently removing someone from the project or community.

Actions will be determined based on the nature and severity of the behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, community channels, and other spaces where participants are representing or participating in the project.

## Contact

If you need to report a Code of Conduct issue, please contact the project maintainers through the project's designated communication channel.
